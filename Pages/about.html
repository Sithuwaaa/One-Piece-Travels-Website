Who We Are

We Believe in the Power of Solo Travel

One Piece Travels was founded in 2020 by a group of passionate solo travellers who believed that working professionals deserved a smarter, more comfortable way to explore Sri Lanka on their own terms.

We started with just two caravans and a dream. Today, we operate a fleet of 12 modern caravans, have served over 500 happy solo travellers, and cover more than 30 destinations across the island.

Our mission is simple — make solo caravan travel accessible, safe and unforgettable for every professional who needs to recharge, rediscover and reconnect with the world around them.
